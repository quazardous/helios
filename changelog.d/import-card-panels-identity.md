A memory card is known by its volume in the import window's Favorites and History too: pinned or imported from at one mount point, it is the same single item when mounted elsewhere, with its last import, and "Import again" reads it where it is now. Another card mounted at the same path is another source. A copy import notes the card's files on the card's volume, so they are "already seen" wherever the card is mounted next.
