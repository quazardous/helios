The panels that follow the photo shown (Scopes, Navigator) say when what they show is not current: from the moment another photo is selected until its data lands, and while an edit's preview renders, their content is dimmed with a busy mark in their corner and on their tab (only after a short delay, so a fast update does not flicker); a failed render dims them with a failed mark that gives the reason. The mark clears with the new data.
