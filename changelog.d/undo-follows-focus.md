Ctrl+Z follows the focused view. In the image, its modules, masks,
parameters, scopes or navigator, it undoes the photo's edit; in the
graph or the Workshops, the workspace's edits; in the Library, the
Library's. It no longer takes whichever step is newest. With the focus
on none of them (the menu bar), it goes to the one focused last.
Ctrl+Shift+Z redoes in the same history.
