"Move to Trash…" (Ctrl+Alt+Shift+Delete) and "Delete Rejected…" (Ctrl+Backspace), as in Lightroom, in the grid's context menu: the photos' files go to the system trash after a question naming the count and the place. A journaled job records each file's outcome and can be resumed. Files on a memory card or a backup volume are never touched, with the reason said. The photos leave the Library; the system trash restores them, Undo does not.
