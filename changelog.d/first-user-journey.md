The bench box's `journey` job walks a new user's first steps headless on a fresh profile: the init wizard on its defaults, a card of raws, JPEGs and RAW+JPEG pairs imported, the Library's count, a quit and a restart, with no error toast, no error or panic in the logs and exit 0. A failure names the step, with its screen and the logs. It runs on every `next` and before a release.
