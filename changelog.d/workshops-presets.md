The experimental Workshops panel is drawn with the theme: text in the theme's roles (group titles as section titles, versions as small print), the "Find" field is the theme's text field, buttons are the theme's (Confirm as the primary action, a row's Apply, Keep, Accept and Reject as small row buttons), the preview switch and "Make it automatic"'s choices are the theme's checkbox and radio buttons, hover texts the theme's tooltips, and the list scrolls in the theme's scroll area.
