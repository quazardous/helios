Library: the search field suggests the keys the word typed starts (`ca` offers "Camera — camera:"; Down and Enter, Tab or a click takes one), and each key term typed in full (a space after it, or Enter) becomes a chip in words ("camera is R5"), the words staying text. Backspace in the empty field selects the last chip and removes it at a second press, Ctrl+Backspace at once; Escape closes the suggestions, then clears the text, then goes back to the grid, the chips kept. The chips' rules are part of the filter the bus's `library.filter` reports and applies; a filter set by a command is shown as text, its chips dropped.
