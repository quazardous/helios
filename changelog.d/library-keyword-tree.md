The Library has a Keywords section: keywords written with `|` levels (`Animals|Dog`) as a tree, each branch with how many photos carry it. A click shows a branch's photos, with the other filters. Drag a keyword onto a photo to tag it, or photos onto a keyword; its menu adds it to or removes it from the selection. Rename a branch, or merge it into another (its menu, or drop it on the other), on every photo at once: one step Undo takes back.
