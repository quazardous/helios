Import from a service's albums: an account whose service lets Helios list its albums shows them under it in the import window's Services panel, page by page ("More albums"). Clicking an album selects it as the source: its photos fill the middle, "in your Library" marked, and Import copies the ones ticked with the account's choices, the history naming the album. Google Photos lists none: its API no longer lets an application read the albums.
