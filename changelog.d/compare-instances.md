Compare opens as many times as you want: the photo menu's "Show in ▸" has "New surface" and "New window", each a new Compare with its own comparison, kept with the layout (tabs "Compare 2", "Compare 3"…). A new double-click choice, "a new Compare", opens a fresh one each time. Agents use `photo.show` with `surface: "new"`, `"new_window"` or `helios.compare#<n>`, and the `compare.*` commands take `surface`.
