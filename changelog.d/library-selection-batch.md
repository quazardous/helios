The Library selects none (Ctrl+Shift+A), inverts the selection (Ctrl+I) and selects the photos similar to the selected ones, taken the same day or in the same stack. A photo's marks (rating, colour labels, tags, title) are copied and pasted onto the selection, merged or overwriting, as darktable's metadata copy: `library.select.*` and `library.marks.copy`, `library.marks.paste`.
