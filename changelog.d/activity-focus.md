The Activity focus: every background job (imports, the trash, exports, whoever started them: you, an agent, the command line) in one window, as two panels. Jobs lists them, running first, with a progress bar when the job says how far it is, filters by kind and state, and "Clear finished". Job detail shows the selected job's source, times, time left, current file, per-file outcome, log, and Cancel, Retry or "Show in Library". A click on the status bar's job opens it on that job; View › Activity and Ctrl+Shift+J too.
