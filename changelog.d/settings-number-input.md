kind: fixed

A number setting with a wide range, such as Files seen kept (up to 100 000 000 files) or Missing files kept (up to 3 650 days), is typed in a field with its unit and small up and down arrows instead of a slider where one pixel was thousands of files. The arrow keys and the wheel step it; a value out of range or not a whole number is refused with its reason. Settings with a short range keep their slider.
