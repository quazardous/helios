Camera look at import: raws of a camera with a default camera look are imported with it, as their first version, undone with the import. The import window's Choices show "Apply the camera's default look", on by default, when the library keeps one. The Camera look header has a Presets list whose "Camera look" puts the camera's default look on, or fits one to the camera's JPEG when the camera has none, as one undoable step.
