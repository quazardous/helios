A memory card names the camera its photos come from: the card connector reads the EXIF make and model of the first few files under `DCIM` (the most common one when they differ), once per card on its own thread, and declares it on the card's source, so the import window shows "Nikon Z 50" and its drawing. The answer is kept for the session: the next listing, and the card plugged again, show it at once.
