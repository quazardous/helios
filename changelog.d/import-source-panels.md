The import window's sources are now in collapsible panels, as in Lightroom: Favourites, History, Folders, Cards and cameras, Services. Every source shows the same way (an icon, its name, one line such as its path, last import or account), and a pin adds it to Favourites. One source is imported at a time: a click selects it, highlighted, and the photos and the detail on the right follow it.
