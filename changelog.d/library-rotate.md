Rotate in the Library: Ctrl+[ and Ctrl+] (as Lightroom and darktable), or the grid's context menu, turn the selected photos a quarter left or right, and "Reset Rotation" turns them back. The grid, filmstrip and Compare show them turned, thumbnails without decoding again; Ctrl+Z turns them back. Through the node's new `library.images.rotate`; commands `library.rotateLeft`, `library.rotateRight`, `library.resetRotation`.
