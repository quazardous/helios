Import window: a known camera (Nikon Z 50, Nikon D750, Canon EOS 5D Mark IV, Sony ILCE-7M3, Fujifilm X-T4 and X100V, Ricoh GR III) is shown in the detail zone by a real photo from Wikimedia Commons, credited under it ("Photo: author, licence, Wikimedia Commons"). Each model's photo is fetched once, off the interface thread, and kept in the user cache folder; only the Commons file's name is sent. Offline, unknown models or before the photo arrives, the drawing stays. A setting, Library > Imports > "Camera photos from Wikimedia Commons", turns it off (drawings only).
