The Library groups the selected photos into a stack (Group into Stack, Ctrl+G), merging the stacks they are in, ungroups them (Unstack, Ctrl+Shift+G) and puts a photo on top of its stack (Move to Top of Stack), from the grid's context menu too. Each is a step that Undo takes back. Switching or removing a version inside a command batch is now part of the batch's single undo step.
