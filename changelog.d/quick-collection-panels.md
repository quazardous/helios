Quick-collection panels: a quick collection's menu (or `library.quick.open`) opens it in a panel of its own, as many as needed. Drag photos from one quick collection to another to move them, one undo step; hold Ctrl to copy. Photos from anywhere else are added, and a photo dragged from a panel onto Compare joins the comparison. Other views keep their cursor, and the panels come back at the next start.
