The import window's "Add…" and "Edit source…" wizard is drawn with the theme: its title and text in the theme's roles (descriptions muted, an account's state as small print, warnings in the warning tone), the folder and name fields are the theme's text fields, its buttons are the theme's (Next, "Add the source" and "Save the source" as the primary action), the kinds, cards and accounts are the theme's selectable rows, the profiles and "Import automatically" the theme's radio buttons, hover texts the theme's tooltips, and the steps scroll in the theme's scroll area.
