The handles on the image (a circle's, an ellipse's or a gradient's mask, and the plugins' tools) take the keyboard: Tab reaches them, the arrows move a point (Shift by a larger step) as one undo step for its x and y, `+` and `-` step a size or an angle, Escape cancels. Screen readers hear a size or an angle as a slider, and a point's x and y.
