Several Library views: View › New Library (or the tab bar's +) opens another Library view, as many as needed. Each has its own filter, folder or album, and its own cursor, with its filters beside its photos; moving in one never moves another. The views come back at the next start as they were left, and closing one leaves the others alone. Agents open and close them with `library.view.open` and `library.view.close`.
