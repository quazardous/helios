The Library has a Metadata section: the capture details of the photo under the cursor (camera, lens, exposure, date), and the title, description, creator, copyright, rating, colour labels, flag and keywords of the whole selection. A field the photos share shows its value, else ‹mixed›; a value typed is set on every selected photo, the others kept. Each edit is one step Undo takes back. Templates keep a set of fields and keywords to apply to a selection.
