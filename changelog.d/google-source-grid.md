kind: changed

Photos chosen in Google Photos no longer import as soon as you press Done there. They now fill the ordinary import grid, with Google's small previews as thumbnails and "in your Library" on the ones you already have. New only and Select all, none or day work as they do for a card, and "Choose more…" adds photos from another visit. The footer's Import downloads and imports them like a card import: progress, a toast and an undo step, with Choices > Into deciding the folder.
