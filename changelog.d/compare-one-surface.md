Compare is one surface for one to sixteen photos (egui-pop 0.14): a single photo fills it as a loupe, more are laid out side by side or in a grid. Drag photos from the Results or the filmstrip onto it to add them where the bar shows, drag a pane's caption to reorder it or out of the surface to take it out, or use its × button or Delete. `compare.add` takes an `at` place.
