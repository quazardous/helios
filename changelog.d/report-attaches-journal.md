Help › Report a problem now shows a diagnostic report before the issue opens: what Helios did in the last 5 minutes (commands, refusals, facts), the shape of the library (how many photos, raw, JPEG and pairs, and what limits the view, never a name or a path), the layout and the settings you changed. Paths are hidden, photos are named `photo-1`, `photo-2`… and private commands read `<private>`. Nothing is sent: the button copies the report and opens the issue page, where you paste it.
