Helios

Photo editing with darktable's quality, without having to learn darktable.

Helios is in alpha: it changes every day and features are missing. Try it on copies of your photos and keep your own backups.

What it is

Helios is a free and open-source photo editor for Linux, Windows and macOS, licensed under the GNU GPL v3. It builds on darktable's image processing and organises it into a library, a darkroom and workshops: small, ready-made sets of adjustments that start from what you want the photo to look like.

Your photos stay yours

Helios runs on your own computer. Your library, your edits and your backups are files on your disks. Helios has no account of its own, no server of its own, and sends nothing about you or your photos to its developers.

Importing from Google Photos

Helios can import photos from your Google Photos account. You sign in with Google, then choose the photos in Google's own picking page; Helios only receives the photos you picked, and copies them into your library folder on your computer. Read the privacy policy for the details.

Getting it

Builds for Linux (AppImage, Flatpak bundle, archive), Windows (installer and archive) and macOS (Apple silicon) are attached to each release. Questions, bugs and ideas go to the project's issue tracker.
