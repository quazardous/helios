Privacy policy

HeliosSource code

Last updated: 13 November 2026.

In short

Helios is a desktop application that runs on your own computer. It has no account of its own and no server of its own. It does not collect, store or share any personal data with its developers or with anyone else. It contains no analytics, no advertising and no tracking.

Data Helios works with

Everything Helios handles stays on your computer, in places you choose: your photos and your library folder, your edits, the library's database (the "vault"), your settings, and the backups you set up. Helios does not send any of it anywhere unless you tell it to, for example by exporting a photo to a folder or by using an online service you connect yourself (such as an AI provider you configure).

Google Photos

If you choose to import from Google Photos:

  • Helios asks Google for these permissions only: photospicker.mediaitems.readonly, which lets Helios read the photos you pick in Google's own picking page, and openid with email, which give the e-mail address of the Google account you signed in with. Helios cannot see the rest of your Google Photos library, your albums or any other account details.
  • The photos you pick are downloaded directly from Google to your computer and copied into your Helios library folder. They are not sent to the developers of Helios or to anyone else.
  • Helios keeps the sign-in tokens Google gives it in your operating system's keyring (password store), on your computer, so you do not have to sign in at each use. It also keeps the account's e-mail address in your library, to show which account is connected (for example "Google Photos — name@example.com"). Nothing else from your Google account is stored.
  • You can disconnect the account in Helios (Settings, Accounts), and revoke Helios's access at any time from your Google account's third-party access page.

Helios's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Children

Helios is a general-purpose photo editor and is not directed at children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version is published on this page with a new date. Its history is public in the project's source repository.
